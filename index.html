About Omar

Ads are a channel.
The business is the context.

I’m Omar Magdy, a Performance Marketer and Media Buyer. I look beyond the ad account to understand the offer, the audience, the customer journey and what the business can deliver.

That context shapes the work: what to say, where to send people, what to measure, and what to change next. Media buying is one part of that system, supported by creative thinking, funnel planning, retargeting and ongoing analysis.

Before you hire a media buyer

Know what the work involves.

Straight answers about preparation, testing and how decisions are made.

Business context, a clear offer, access to the relevant ad account and tracking, and an understanding of what happens after a lead or sale. We identify any gaps before launch.

It depends on the market, objective, offer and the signal volume needed to learn. We discuss a test budget against those factors instead of using one figure for every business.

We need suitable creative inputs for the offer and audience. The plan can include direction on messages and formats to prepare before campaigns go live.

Testing starts with a clear question, such as which audience or message better supports the objective. We review the resulting signals in context and use them to choose the next step.

We inspect the objective, tracking, audience, creative and destination experience to find where the journey may be breaking down. Then we agree on the next test or adjustment.

Retargeting can be part of the strategy when the available audience, tracking and customer journey make it useful.

The objective and business model determine the useful measures. Platform metrics need to be considered alongside lead quality, sales process and the outcome the business cares about.

Advertising results depend on the offer, market, creative, budget and customer experience. I can explain the plan, measurement and decisions; a specific outcome cannot be guaranteed in advance.

Scaling is considered when results are sufficiently consistent, tracking is dependable, and the business can handle additional demand. The decision follows the evidence, not a preset promise.
