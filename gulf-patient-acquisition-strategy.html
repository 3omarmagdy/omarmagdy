Strategy case study · Healthcare

Designing a patient acquisition journey for Gulf markets.

A proposed strategic plan for a healthcare provider exploring cross-border patient enquiries. The case study focuses on the thinking, funnel and measurement approach; the client is not named to protect project privacy.

Proposed strategy · Not a reported campaignResearch → Funnel → Measurement

Challenge

Plan a clear path for prospective patients considering treatment away from home, where trust, education, response quality and practical travel questions all affect whether an enquiry becomes a suitable consultation.

Business context

The source plan explored Gulf demand for specialist dental care in Egypt. Service mix, clinical capacity, average case value and operating inputs needed confirmation before launch.

Initial diagnosis

  • A paid campaign alone would not resolve trust and qualification gaps.
  • Patient education and a reliable enquiry response process needed to support acquisition.
  • Capacity and commercial assumptions had to be validated before setting scale expectations.

Strategic insight

Build the journey around a useful first conversation and clear next steps. Treat qualified consultations and operational readiness as the goal, while using advertising to identify demand and learn which messages merit further investment.

Audience & creative direction

Prioritize relevant geographic markets and intent signals, then use doctor-led educational content to answer common treatment and travel questions. Creative concepts would be reviewed for clinical accuracy and applicable advertising requirements before use.

Funnel strategy

  • Introduce the service through educational paid content and search.
  • Route interested people to a clear enquiry and consultation path.
  • Qualify enquiries with an agreed response process and clinician review.
  • Track later journey stages so optimization reflects business quality, not clicks alone.

Campaign planning & optimization

The plan proposed a staged rollout: confirm service, capacity, tracking and response readiness; run controlled channel and creative tests; then review qualified enquiry signals before making any scale decision. Retargeting would be used only where audience consent, tracking and platform rules support it.

What this plan is designed to learn

Which market and message combinations create suitable enquiries, where prospects need more information, and whether the consultation and follow-up process can support demand. The source document treats its forecasts as assumptions to validate—not benchmarks or achieved outcomes.

Results

This is a strategy showcase, not a report of an executed campaign. No spend, leads, patient volume, revenue, conversion rate or return-on-ad-spend result is being claimed.
